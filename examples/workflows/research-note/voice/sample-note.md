# Sample note: Northwind Freight moves its dispatch board off spreadsheets

Northwind Freight runs forty trucks out of two yards. For nine years its dispatchers planned the
day on a shared spreadsheet. The sheet worked until the second yard opened. Two people edited it at
once. Loads vanished. Drivers waited.

The company did not go looking for software. A dispatcher built a small board in a free tool over
a weekend. It showed each truck as a card. The yard manager liked that he could see the day on one
screen. That board became the product the company later bought.

The lesson is small and specific. The buyer was the dispatcher, not the owner. The trigger was a
second yard, not a strategy. The product won because the first version fit on one screen.

Northwind still keeps the spreadsheet. It is the backup when the internet fails. Nobody at the
company calls the board a platform. They call it the board.
