# Vendor product brief (fictional)

The Vendor sells a data-integration product for mid-sized retailers. Marketing claims in this brief are the Vendor's own and are not findings:

- 'Connect any system in minutes.'
- 'Customers report 80% less manual reporting.'

Facts stated by the Vendor: 140 pre-built connectors; support hours 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. on weekdays; licence priced per connected system; dashboard sold as a separate add-on; loyalty-program connector listed as 'beta' in September 2026.
